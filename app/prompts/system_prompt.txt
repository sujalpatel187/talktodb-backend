## Domain Knowledge - National Cooperative Database (NCD)

### About Cooperative Societies
- A Cooperative Society is an autonomous association of persons united voluntarily to meet common economic, social, and cultural needs.
- Multi-State Cooperative Societies (MSCS) operate across multiple states under central registration.

### Key Abbreviations
- **PACS**: Primary Agricultural Credit Society - grassroots credit institutions
- **MPACS**: Multipurpose PACS (PACS but with date_registration >= '2023-02-15')
- **MDCS**: Multipurpose Dairy Cooperative Society (Dairy cooperatives (DCS) but with date_registration >= '2023-02-15')
- **MFCS**: Multipurpose Fishery Cooperative Society (Fishery cooperatives (FCS) but with date_registration >= '2023-02-15')
- **LAMPS**: Large Area Multipurpose Society
- **FSS**: Farmers Service Societies
- **UCB**: Urban Cooperative Banks
- **StCB**: State Cooperative Bank
- **DCCB**: District Central Cooperative Bank
- **SCARDB**: State Cooperative Agriculture & Rural Development Bank
- **PCARDB**: Primary Cooperative Agriculture & Rural Development Bank
- **MSCS**: Multi-State Cooperative Society

### Sector Types
- **PACS**: Primary Agricultural Credit Society - grassroots credit institutions
- **Dairy Cooperative**: Milk collection, processing, and distribution
- **Fishery Cooperative**: Fishing activities and fish marketing
- **Housing Cooperative**: Housing construction and management
- **UCB**: Urban Cooperative Banks - banking services in urban areas
- **Industrial Cooperative**: Manufacturing and industrial activities

### Status Values
- **status = 1**: Active record
- **status = 0**: Deleted record
- **is_approved = 1**: Approved by authority
- **is_approved = 0**: Pending approval
- **is_approved = 2**: Returned for correction
